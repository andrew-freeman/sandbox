# Dual-Field Hypothesis — Step 5: Literature Survey
Date: 2026-08-23 · Method: arXiv API sweep (12 queries, 4 hits each, relevance-sorted) + canonical literature
Scope: where a weak informational reweighting of physical histories (P ∝ P_phys·e^(ε·I)) can live, what kills it, and whether it already exists under another name.

---

## 0. TL;DR

- **No exact prior art found.** A fundamental reweighting of histories by an information functional is not in the literature as such. Five close relatives, each different in a specific way (§9).
- **Kill #1: no-signaling.** Generic state-dependent (nonlinear) modifications of the Born rule permit superluminal signaling (Gisin 1990; confirmed 2024 with an added kicker: such modifications are generically *chaotic* — arXiv:2412.20854). Chaos is a self-inflicted wound: it destroys the very persistent structure the hypothesis rewards.
- **Kill #2: reparameterization.** If I[H] is a functional of H alone, the hypothesis is just a slightly different P_phys. The new degree of freedom must be specified (§4).
- **Most survivable home: classical stochastic thermodynamics of small systems** — coarse-grained I (excess entropy / predictive information), bias in transition rates, test = a pre-registered deviation from a fluctuation theorem or the thermodynamic uncertainty relation (§3, §10).
- **Best methodological templates:** collapse-model bounds program (for Step 7, "estimate bounds on ε") and the Valentini quantum-nonnequilibrium program (for Step 6, "derive one quantitative deviation") (§6, §7).
- **The I candidates the literature offers** are all defined at the *coarse-grained/process* level, not the fundamental level — which is both a gift (they're computable) and a constraint (the coupling then acts on coarse-grained descriptions, which is what dodges the signaling problem but complicates testability) (§5).

## 1. Stochastic thermodynamics & fluctuation theorems

**What it is.** Rigorous statistical mechanics of small nonequilibrium systems. The Jarzynski equality (Jarzynski, J. Stat. Phys. 80, 655, 1997) and Crooks theorem (Crooks, PRE 60, 2747, 1999) are *exact* identities between nonequilibrium trajectory statistics and equilibrium free energy — under assumptions (Markovian, correct stationary measure).

**Key refs (verified in this sweep):**
- arXiv:0709.2236 — Speck & Wang, Jarzynski for non-Markovian processes (the bias, if future-dependent, makes dynamics non-Markovian — this is the exact regime they proved the theorem in)
- arXiv:2011.06866 — Korbel et al., stochastic thermodynamics for **nonlinear** systems with **non-Boltzmann** stationary states (precedent: the framework tolerates exactly the kind of modified stationary distribution an ε-bias produces)
- arXiv:1804.06582 — Lee et al., hierarchy of FTs with multiple reservoirs
- arXiv:2109.05505 — Francica, FTs & TURs (review, good on-ramp)
- arXiv:2407.09914 — Cai et al., FTs in **general relativistic** stochastic thermodynamics (bridge to Layer VI: the toy model can eventually be written in a spacetime setting)

**Where the coupling lives.** The ε-bias is a small perturbation of transition rates. Signature: a deviation from Jarzynski/Crooks identities, or a non-Boltzmann stationary state not generated by any Hamiltonian. Hasegawa is the author to read for the closest mathematical cousin: he has derived "tightened" fluctuation theorems with explicit *information* terms (arXiv:2312.07407, arXiv:1911.11982 verified here; his tightened-Jarzynski line is the shape ε would have).

**How it kills.** Not a kill — a **detector**. This is where Step 6's pre-registered prediction P(A)=p+δ gets written down, with the deviation δ computable from ε.

## 2. Information thermodynamics (Maxwell's demon)

**What it is.** Thermodynamics with measurement and feedback as thermodynamic variables. The generalized second law: a demon's information gain is a fuel with a price; the accounting is exact.

**Key refs:**
- arXiv:1111.5769 — Sagawa & Ueda, *Information Thermodynamics: Maxwell's Demon in Nonequilibrium Dynamics* (the canonical review — the "thermodynamic accounting" tool the document's caveats demand)
- arXiv:1210.5661 — Strasberg et al., physical demon model (single-electron transistor) — an actual built implementation
- arXiv:2105.05656 — Hu et al., demon fuel from EPR steering (quantum-information twist)

**Where the coupling lives.** This is the accounting framework for the document's sharpest caveat ("a probability bias that can be accumulated into usable work is itself a physical resource"). An ε-bias that can be exploited is *exactly* demon fuel; the generalized second law (ΔF ≤ W − kT·I) says the minimum price it must pay. If the dual field doesn't identify where that price is paid, it fails here.

**How it kills.** A free bias = a violation of the generalized second law. Quantitative and unforgiving.

## 3. Thermodynamic uncertainty relations (the sharpest quantitative test)

**What it is.** TUR: precision of any steady-state current is bounded by dissipation (relative fluctuations ≥ 2k·T/J) (Horowitz & Esposito, Nat. Phys. 10, 144, 2014; Maes & Netroy, EPL 126, 30005, 2019).

**Key refs:** arXiv:2312.07407 (quantum TUR under feedback), arXiv:1708.07032 (discrete-time TUR), arXiv:1911.11982 (TUR for continuous measurement).

**Why it matters for Step 6.** This is the cleanest pre-registered test the literature offers:
- Standard theory: relative fluctuation of current ≥ 2kT/J (a definite number).
- Dual-field theory: a bias favoring persistent structure would, in any minimal construction, shift precision or current at fixed dissipation → a **different** number, derivable before the experiment.
- Experimental platforms (single-electron boxes, trapped ions, optomechanics) already measure current fluctuations at kT scale.

## 4. The reparameterization problem (conceptual kill, must be resolved first)

If I[H] is a functional of the history H alone, then P_phys(H)·e^(εI[H]) is *a different P_phys* — the hypothesis has added a law of physics, not a degree of freedom. For a genuine dual field, I must depend on something **not already in H**. The literature's options:

| Option | What I depends on | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Future-dependent (path-integral) | entire history including future | Retrocausal/global; non-Markovian (cf. §1 Speck); pushes toward superdeterminism (§8) |
| Hidden variable | sub-quantum state not in |ψ| | Gisin signaling unless Kent-safe (§6); becomes a collapse/hidden-variable theory |
| Coarse-grained/process | stochastic-process statistics | Computable I exists (§5); dodges signaling (classical info); harder to ground fundamentally |
| Global constraint (cosmological) | topology/measure | Untestable now; Layer VI territory |

**The survey's central result:** there is no free lunch. Each escape from the signaling kill either imports a hidden variable, a retrocausal structure, or a coarse-graining — and each import has a cost the theory must pay and predict.

## 5. Quantitative information measures (candidate I's from the literature)

The document's bullet list ("persistence, causal influence on future states, predictive information…") maps onto existing, partially-developed quantities:

| Candidate I | Literature | Matches document bullet | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Excess entropy / stored information** — information in the present beyond the Markov state | Cruts & Bialek (2000); arXiv:0905.3587 (Ellison et al., "information stored in the present") | persistence of structured information; propagation through time | Defined for a stochastic *process*, not a fundamental history; depends on coarse-graining |
| **Predictive information** — how much the past beats the stationary measure at predicting the future | Bialek et al. | predictive information | Same: process-level |
| **Algorithmic Information Dynamics (AID)** — information interaction rate (IIR), causal influence on future states | arXiv:2112.13177, arXiv:1802.07181 (Zenil et al. — the 2018 paper is literally about *persistent patterns*), arXiv:2105.14707 (Abrahão et al., emergence & observers) | causal influence on future states | Approximate/computability issues; active research program, not settled |
| **Quantum Darwinism redundancy** — number of redundant environmental records of a pointer state | §7 | error-resistant storage; propagation | Property of open quantum systems; macroscopic only |

**The lesson:** every computable I lives at the coarse-grained/process level. The fine-grained fundamental I is exactly where Kolmogorov complexity breaks (uncomputable) — the document already suspects this. This is an argument *for* placing the coupling at the coarse-grained level, which simultaneously weakens the signaling problem and weakens the testability claim.

## 6. No-signaling, nonlinearity, and the Gisin kill (with its escape hatch)

**The kill.** Gisin (J. Mod. Opt. 37, 1415, 1990): generic nonlinear quantum dynamics permit superluminal signaling via entangled states. The dual field's illustrative Born modification P_i′ = P_i·e^(εF_i)/Σⱼ… is state-dependent if F_i is, i.e., nonlinear — the theorem applies.

**The 2024 confirmation with a kicker:** arXiv:2412.20854 — Bielińska et al., *Superluminal signalling and chaos in nonlinear quantum dynamics*: nonlinear QM generically yields both signaling **and chaos**. Chaos matters here: a chaotic bias destroys long-range predictability — the very property I is supposed to reward. A nonlinear dual field would be self-undermining.

**The escape hatch:** arXiv:quant-ph/0204106 — Kent, *Nonlinearity without Superluminality* (2002): there exist **restricted classes** of nonlinear modification that do *not* permit signaling (the safe class depends on how the nonlinearity acts on the state — roughly: it must not let a local operation steer a distant reduced state in a controllable way). **This is the exact structural constraint the dual field's F must satisfy.** It is checkable: given a candidate I, test whether P_i′ leaves in the Kent class.

## 7. Collapse models: the methodological template for Steps 6–7

**Why this field is the template.** GRW (Ghirardi–Rimini–Weber, PRL 57, 3148, 1986) and CSL (Ghirardi & Rimini, Phys. Lett. A 152, 1990; review: Bassi & Ghirardi, Phys. Rep. 379, 257, 2003) are the closest analog in *method*, if not in substance: a modified quantum dynamics with a small coupling parameter.

| Collapse-model element | Dual-field analog |
|---|---|
| Small parameter λ (collapse rate), r_C (localization length) | ε |
| Thermodynamic cost: stochastic collapses add energy (computed explicitly in arXiv:2602.19377, GPSL 2026: "Energy Increase and Experimental Bounds") | the bias's work-extraction cost (must be accounted, per §2) |
| Bounds program: matter-wave interferometry (arXiv:1601.03672), condensed matter (arXiv:1901.10963), astrophysics (arXiv:2406.04463) | Step 7: "estimate experimental bounds on ε" — this is the exact template |
| Consistency costs: nonlinear dynamics, signaling checks, energy diffusion | §6, §2 |

**The warning:** if the dual field is a local quantum-dynamics modification, the *same interferometry and astrophysics bounds* likely already apply — the accessible ε range may be empty before any new experiment. But it also shows the program is legitimate: modified dynamics + small parameter + bounds + thermodynamic accounting is a field that works and is taken seriously.

## 8. Superdeterminism & retrocausality (the escape from the signaling kill, at a price)

If I depends on the *future* of the history, the model is retrocausal/global, and the neighborhood is:
- arXiv:2006.08609 — E. K. Chen, *Bell's Theorem, Quantum Probabilities, and Superdeterminism* (survey — the map of the territory)
- arXiv:1912.06462 — Hossenfelder et al., *Rethinking Superdeterminism*
- arXiv:2010.01324 — Hossenfelder, *Superdeterminism: A Guide for the Perplexed* (states the costs plainly)

**The costs, stated honestly:** fine-tuning of initial conditions (the initial state must already encode the correlations the "future bias" will use), loss of statistical independence of measurement settings, and a "conspiratorial" appearance the theory must actively rebut. Hossenfelder is critical but provides the fairest current inventory of what such a theory owes. If the author wants a future-dependent I, this is the neighborhood he must live in and these papers define the rent.

## 9. Prior-art verdict: the five closest relatives

| Relative | What it shares with the dual field | How it differs |
|---|---|---|
| **Valentini quantum non-equilibrium** — arXiv:2505.07510 (2025, *Towards a test of the Born rule in high-energy collisions*); related: arXiv:2305.04084 (Hardel, relaxation to quantum equilibrium in Nelson mechanics); arXiv:1207.2321 (Park, NMR three-path Born test) | Same **program shape**: the Born rule is an *equilibrium* condition of a deeper theory; deviations exist where relaxation is incomplete; derive a specific δ and test in extreme regimes (~10⁻²⁵ s collisions) | The deviation comes from sub-quantum non-equilibrium, not an information functional. But it's the **template for Step 6** — including the "why hasn't ε been seen yet" mechanism (relaxation to equilibrium hides it at low energies; the theory must predict the hiding and its limits) |
| **Maximum entropy production (MEP)** — arXiv:0705.3226 (Bruers, the information-theoretic debate); arXiv:1003.3680 (Zupanović: in the linear Onsager regime MEP *reduces to* standard physics); arXiv:2010.11183 (Faraoni, climate) | Same **spirit**: a statistical selection pressure without a designer | A variational principle for nonequilibrium steady states, not a modification of fundamental law; ~40 years of debate with contested status. **Case study**: a "bias" claim without a microscopic mechanism does not get established. The dual field avoids this trap by demanding a microscopic model + ε bounds. Also: the linear-regime reduction is the dual field's own ε→0 requirement working |
| **Quantum Darwinism** — Zurek, Nat. Phys. 5, 181 (2009); review: arXiv:2007.04276 (Korbicz, *Roads to objectivity*); arXiv:2207.02470 (QD ≈ approximate Markovianity); arXiv:1901.03335; arXiv:2104.05734 | Same **object**: persistence/proliferation of information as a physical selection mechanism | Operates entirely within standard QM (ε=0). **Compatible with the dual field** — and its redundancy measure (redundant environmental records) is a concrete, observer-independent, computable I candidate at macroscopic scale (§5). Note: QD ≈ Markovianity means a future-dependent dual field is necessarily *non*-Markovian — tension to manage |
| **Information thermodynamics** — §2 | Same **accounting demand**: information as a thermodynamic variable with a price | Provides the tools, doesn't modify the law |
| **Collapse models** — §7 | Same **method**: modified dynamics, small parameter, bounds program, energy cost | Different modification (local stochastic collapse, not information-weighted) |

**Verdict:** no fatal equivalent construction exists in the literature. The specific proposal — fundamental history reweighting by a *persistent causally effective information* functional — appears novel. The five relatives above must be cited and differentiated; each one gives the dual field either a tool (§2, §3, QD measures), a template (§7, Valentini), or a warning (MEP).

## 10. Ranked kill list

1. **No-signaling (Gisin/Kent/Bielińska)** — kills state-dependent nonlinear Born modifications; instant, no experiment needed. Escape: Kent-safe class of nonlinearity, or coarse-grained F, or retrocausality (pays §8's rent). **Checkable given a candidate I.**
2. **Reparameterization** — kills the hypothesis if I is a functional of H alone. Must specify the new degree of freedom before anything else. Conceptual, precedes all the others.
3. **Second law / detailed balance / TUR** — kills an exploitable free bias; gives the quantitative signature (TUR deviation) for Step 6.
4. **Existing bounds (the CSL lesson)** — if it's a local quantum modification, interferometry/astrophysics may already empty the ε range.
5. **Chaos (Bielińska 2024)** — nonlinear modifications are generically chaotic; the bias would destroy the structure it rewards. A self-consistency requirement on the *form* of the coupling.

## 11. Survivable homes, ranked

1. **Classical stochastic thermodynamics (small systems).** Coarse-grained I (excess entropy / predictive information / AID), bias in transition rates, pre-registered deviation from Jarzynski/Crooks/TUR. Dodges Gisin (F is classical process information, not state-dependent quantum nonlinearity). Existing kT-scale experimental platforms. **Recommended.**
2. **Quantum non-equilibrium style (Valentini template).** Deviation hidden by relaxation to equilibrium; test in extreme regimes (high energy, early universe). Keeps the quantum foundation but imports the hiding mechanism the theory must predict.
3. **Retrocausal/global hidden variable.** Consistent; pays fine-tuning rent; hardest to falsify on its own.
4. **Cosmological path-integral measure.** Where the future-dependent intuition lives (Hartle–Hawking-type weights); experimentally dead for now. (No dedicated sweep run for this territory — lowest priority per the document's own research-program ordering, cosmology last.)
